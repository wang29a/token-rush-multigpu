**Bottleneck:** the wait-each structure does not scale with depth: it is
9.703 us/step at depth 1 and
14.318 us at depth 128 (1.47x), while
tail-wait, same-stream and both consumer chains stay flat
(0.96x, 0.92x,
0.89x). The recorded graphs show why this is a capture
artifact, not transport: the wait-each graph has 258
nodes like the others but 8384 edges against
383 for tail-wait - every all-reduce kernel
depends on every earlier one, while the event-bearing variants each reuse one
CUDA event object. A wait issued on a re-recorded shared event is consistent
with linking each new operation to all previous records, and the added edges
grow quadratically with depth. Inserting a consumer kernel on the capturing stream breaks the pattern
into a linear chain (765 edges,
511 for same-stream). Measured replay time
follows that structure. **Inference, stated as such:** the edge explosion is
the cause; the exact CUDA capture rule that produces it is not documented here
and was not instrumented beyond the DOT dump.

114.48 tok/s, so the collective's
rounding is not the cause. The sharded bf16 GEMMs round their partial sums
differently from the full-width GEMM, and the residual stream accumulates those
roundings for 36 layers. That is enough to flip a near-tie in greedy decoding;
it is a precision property of bf16 TP, not a structural error in the sharding.

Both throughput predictions were too optimistic: the model runs slower than the
component arithmetic suggested, most likely because half-width GEMMs at M=1 do
not reach the bandwidth of the single wide GEMV measured in S4. The speedup
range held; the text-equivalence prediction did not.

**Gate and decision:** S6's speed criterion (>= 1.4x) is met at
1.46x, but **S6 is not passed**: a TP engine whose greedy text leaves
TP=1 at token 4 is not equivalent. No 1.5x claim is made and M1
is not started. The next bounded step is an fp32 residual stream (accumulate
`h` in fp32, cast only at GEMM inputs), then a single re-run of this exact
comparison; if text still diverges, the engine needs fp32 master weights or a
different reduction strategy before any model-level claim.

This also calibrates the project: the measured L is small, but at bs=1 the
measured two-card speedup on the test model is 1.46x, not the 1.45x the
component projection suggested. Test-model numbers do not transfer to 27B.
